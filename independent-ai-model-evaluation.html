Why independent evaluation matters

AI systems are often judged by internal tests, marketing claims, or isolated demos. Independent AI model evaluation creates a separate record that can be inspected, compared, and reused across teams.

Norynthe focuses on practical signals: whether the model follows the task, whether it can be trusted in repeatable workflows, and whether the result can be explained to a buyer, reviewer, or oversight group.

Outside-the-model scoring

Signals are recorded separately from the model provider so review does not depend on a self-reported claim.

Comparable records

Evaluations are structured to make model-to-model and run-to-run comparison easier to understand.

Governed review

Assessment records support repeatable review, escalation, and readiness decisions.
