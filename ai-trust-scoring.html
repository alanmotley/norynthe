Why trust scores need context

A score should simplify comparison without hiding the reason.

FAQ

AI trust scoring questions.

What is AI trust scoring?

It converts model evaluation evidence into a structured score and trust band for comparison and review.

Does a trust score replace evaluation detail?

No. The score is the public signal; the underlying record explains benchmark context and behavior evidence.

Why use a score at all?

Scores make model credibility easier to discuss across buyers, institutions, analysts, and model teams.
