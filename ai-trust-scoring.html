Trust score inputs

  • Evaluation outcomes from repeatable benchmark records.
  • Evidence quality, uncertainty handling, and traceability.
  • Operational readiness for business and oversight use.

A trust layer outside the model

AI trust scoring should not be limited to a provider claim or a single benchmark number. Norynthe treats the trust score as an external record built from evidence, model behavior, and governance context.

This makes the score easier to cite, compare, and explain when AI systems are being reviewed for procurement, product release, or internal deployment.

Evidence signal

Scores can reflect whether a model supports answers with traceable, useful evidence.

Reliability signal

Scores can summarize consistency across model runs, tasks, and review conditions.

Readiness signal

Scores can help teams understand whether a model is ready for a defined use case.

Where trust scoring helps

Norynthe.Score is designed for moments where teams need a clear, defensible AI confidence signal rather than a long technical appendix.

  • Comparing model options during AI vendor selection.
  • Tracking trust signals as models change over time.
  • Creating public or internal score boards for AI assurance.
  • Supporting board, legal, compliance, and executive review.

Frequently asked questions

What is AI trust scoring?

AI trust scoring converts evaluation evidence, reliability signals, and governance context into a clear score or rating for review.

Is trust scoring the same as model performance scoring?

No. Performance is one input. Trust scoring also considers evidence quality, repeatability, risk context, and readiness for a specific use.

Why should trust scoring be external?

An external trust layer can be compared and cited without relying only on the model provider claim.
